Got a Letter from MVD?

Follow these steps to understand your letter and what to do next.

  1. 1

    Sign in to AZ MVD Now

    No account? Sign up (opens in a new tab) first.

  2. 2

    Find your letter

    Go to Your Account › Your Documents.

  3. 3

    Scan the QR code

    If your letter has one, scan it for answers to common questions.

  4. 4

    Review your compliance issues

    Go to Other › Manage Compliance Issues.
